## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-10

The first release. For Windows 10 and later, 64-bit, as a setup program and
as a zip. Neither is signed.

- Accounts and credit cards, income and spending, and transactions dated in
  the future that apply themselves on their day.
- Debts in installments, recurring payments and income, savings goals, each
  with an automatic option that records what was missed on the day it was due.
- A portfolio of shares, gold, currencies and crypto at live prices.
- A budget plan, a calendar, calculators, insights, what-if projections and
  the balance on any past day.
- Records encrypted at rest, a password-protected profile, encrypted backup
  and restore, CSV export and import.
- English and Turkish, dark and light.
